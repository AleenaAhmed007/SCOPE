# Official source extract: FAR 52.246-14 — Inspection of transportation

- Source: U.S. Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR), Acquisition.gov
- Authority: U.S. federal procurement regulation; clause is for specified domestic freight transportation services
- Current page checked: 2026-11-14; page shows FAC 2026-01, effective 2026-03-13
- Official source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.246-14

## Applicability and exclusions

This clause covers listed rail, motor, domestic freight-forwarder, and domestic water-carrier transportation services. The page says not to use this clause for domestic or international air carriers, international ocean carriers, or freight services provided under bills of lading.

## Inspection rights and assistance

The Government may inspect and test the contractor’s services, facilities, and equipment at reasonable times. The contractor must provide Government representatives free access and reasonable facilities and assistance for those inspections and tests.

## Source text excerpt

“The Government has the right to inspect and test the Contractor’s services, facilities, and equipment at all reasonable times.” The contractor must furnish free access and reasonable facilities and assistance required for inspections and tests.
