# Official source extract: FAR 52.247-14 — Contractor responsibility for receipt of shipment

- Source: U.S. Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR), Acquisition.gov
- Authority: U.S. federal procurement regulation; applies when inserted in a covered transportation contract
- Current page checked: 2026-11-14; page shows FAC 2026-01, effective 2026-03-13
- Official source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.247-14

## Applicability

The clause is prescribed for solicitations and contracts for transportation or transportation-related services.

## Receipt and examination

The clause requires the contractor to count and examine all goods tendered for shipment, receipt for them, and make an appropriate written exception for goods not in apparent good order.

## Source text excerpt

“The Contractor shall diligently count and examine all goods tendered for shipment, receipt for them, and make appropriate written exception for any goods not in apparent good order.”
