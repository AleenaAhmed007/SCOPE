# Official source extract: FAR 47.303-8 — F.A.S. vessel, port of shipment

- Source: U.S. Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR), Acquisition.gov
- Authority: U.S. federal procurement regulation; not a universal or SCOPE policy
- Current page checked: 2026-11-14; page shows FAC 2026-01, effective 2026-03-13
- Official source: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/47.303-8
- Companion contract clause: FAR 52.247-36, https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.247-36

## Delivery term

“F.a.s. vessel, port of shipment” means delivery alongside the ocean vessel and within reach of its loading tackle at the specified port of shipment, without expense to the Government.

## Contractor responsibilities

The contractor packs and marks the shipment according to contract specifications, or follows carrier requirements when specifications are absent. The contractor delivers the shipment in good order and condition at the specified point and contract date or period, and bears applicable charges up to that point. The contractor provides a clean dock or ship’s receipt and is responsible for loss or damage before delivery to the specified point. At the Government’s request and expense, the contractor assists with export or import documents.

## Source text excerpts

The official clause states that the contractor shall “provide a clean dock or ship’s receipt”; be “responsible for any loss of and/or damage to the goods occurring before delivery” to the specified point; and, at Government request and expense, assist in obtaining documents required for exportation or importation.
